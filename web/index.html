Connect a wallet holding Squeeks (wallet or staked).

Rules

  1. Only Squeek holders play. Staked Squeeks count too.
  2. Seize the Seed with one Squeek. Your reign earns from the round after you seize, so nobody can claim work that already happened.
  3. Every unit of real wheel work in your reign = 1 point for your Squeek, read from SqueekCore once the round is certified.
  4. A level-L Squeek is shielded for L rounds. Higher levels hold longer.
  5. A dethroned Squeek rests for 6 rounds (1 hour). You can't take the Seed from yourself.
  6. Points stay with the Squeek, so they go with it if you sell it.

Reigns

Anyone can credit a reign once SqueekCore certifies its rounds. Points always go to the reigning Squeek.
