HOW IT WORKS

  • Six floors across three biomes. Every run is a brand-new burrow: caverns, dug-out rooms or warrens, a random start and random loot.
  • Fight two ways. Bump to melee. Sling pebbles from range (Shift+direction / IJKL / 🎯). Crits hit double.
  • Open chests for gear: Thorn Dagger, Pencil Spear (pierces), Acorn Hammer, Golden Fork; Walnut Helm, Thimble Mail, Chestnut Plate; Rubber Sling, Pea Shooter; plus vitamins, clovers, lucky whiskers and pebble pouches. Worse gear is salvaged for seeds.
  • Enemies: beetles, snakes, fast rats (2 steps a turn), tanky slow moles, and the Tabby boss guarding the golden wheel. Each stairway down lets you rest for 25% HP.
  • The real hamster sets each 2-hour expedition's mood from his certified work in the 6 rounds before it opened: 💤 dark & drowsy, 🐾 normal, 🏃 richer, ⚡ deadlier but double seeds.
  • Ranked runs: your Squeek draws a fresh burrow onchain (3 per expedition), you play, and the contract replays every move to score it. Your Squeek's level sets HP/ATK; its art colors your hero.
Sepolia testnet only. No tokens, no money: just seeds, glory and a real hamster.
